# Bill Version Codes

## Overview
As a bill moves through Congress, it goes through several stages. Each stage produces a new version of the bill, marked with a specific code. These codes help track the bill's progress through the legislative process.

## Version Codes and Their Meanings

### House Bills

- `ih` (Introduced in House)
  - This is the first official version of a bill when it is submitted to the House of Representatives
  - Contains the original text as proposed by the bill's sponsor(s)
  - Has not yet been debated or modified by any committees

- `rh` (Reported in House)
  - Version produced after a House committee reviews and possibly amends the bill
  - Includes any changes made by the committee
  - Contains the committee's recommendation on whether the full House should consider the bill

- `eh` (Engrossed in House)
  - The final version passed by the full House of Representatives
  - Includes all amendments adopted during floor debate
  - This is the official copy sent to the Senate for consideration

### Senate Bills

- `rcs` (Reference in Committee Senate)
  - Version of a House-passed bill that has been referred to Senate committee(s)
  - Marks the start of the Senate's formal consideration of the bill
  - May include preliminary Senate committee annotations or references

- `rs` (Reported in Senate)
  - Version produced after a Senate committee reviews and possibly amends the bill
  - Includes any changes recommended by the Senate committee
  - Contains the committee's recommendation for full Senate consideration

- `es` (Engrossed in Senate)
  - The final version passed by the full Senate
  - Includes all amendments adopted during Senate floor debate
  - If different from House version, must be reconciled before becoming law

### Final Stage

- `enr` (Enrolled Bill)
  - The final, official version passed in identical form by both the House and Senate
  - Certified by House/Senate officials as the exact text both chambers approved
  - This version is sent to the President for signature or veto

## Common Progression
A typical bill might follow this sequence:
1. `ih` → `rh` → `eh` (House process)
2. `rcs` → `rs` → `es` (Senate process)
3. `enr` (Final version if passed by both chambers)

Note: Not every bill will have all versions. Some bills may stop at any stage in the process, and the sequence may vary depending on whether the bill originates in the House or Senate.
